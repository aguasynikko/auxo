# Ratings attach to the album, track ratings to the recording

A rating belongs to the album (MusicBrainz release-group), never to an edition, so original, deluxe and remaster ratings all land on one album page. A track rating belongs to the recording, so a song keeps its ratings across every edition that uses the same recording. A remaster with new recording IDs starts its tracks at zero; we accept that rather than fuzzy-match recordings.

Track ratings never change an album's average rating. The average comes only from album ratings, so "4★ album" keeps one meaning. Track ratings drive the rating curve only.
