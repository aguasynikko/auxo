auxo, three directions

Each direction mocks Home and an album page in light and dark, with the Liquid Glass tab bar and nav bar, the wordmark and the app icon. The phones scroll: scroll one down to see the large title give way and the tab bar shrink.

Albums, artists, users and ratings are sample content. Covers are drawn stand-ins.
