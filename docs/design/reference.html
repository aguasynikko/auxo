auxo design reference

Catalogue is the default look. Sleeve and Standard are themes people can pick in Settings: they swap colours, star colour, glass tint, the display face and corner radii over the same layout. Light and dark follow the system in every theme. DESIGN.md is the written spec; this page is what it describes.

Albums, artists, users and ratings are sample content. Covers are drawn stand-ins.

Screens

Home, the album page (at rest and scrolled to the tracklist) and Settings. Scroll a phone down: the large title gives way to an inline one and the tab bar shrinks to the current tab and the Log button.

Web app, wide screens

From 1024px wide (iPad landscape, laptops, desktop browsers) the tab bar becomes a glass sidebar with the wordmark and the Log button, Home splits into two columns, and the album page keeps the cover and your rating beside the details.

Mode

Wordmark and icon

One brand mark in every theme: lowercase auxo in heavy sans with the ultramarine series square. Source files live in docs/design/brand/.

Share cards

Milestone 2 templates. Exported at 1080 × 1920 (story) and 1080 × 1080 (square). Cards always use the Catalogue look, in the sharer’s light or dark mode, whatever theme they picked.

Refraction (the lens bend at the glass edges) renders only in Chromium browsers. On iPhone Safari, and with “Safari look”, the glass is blur and tint only.
